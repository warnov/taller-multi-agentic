Purgar Ai Foundry eliminados:

1. Detectar Foundry en soft delete:
az cognitiveservices account list-deleted --query "[].{name:name, rg:resourceGroup, location:location, kind:kind}" -o table

2. Purgar Foundry:
az cognitiveservices account purge --name ais-contosoretail-re4w5 --resource-group rg-contoso-retail --location eastus
